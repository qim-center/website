This workshop brings together researchers, developers, and imaging specialists working with advanced 3D datasets. The program focuses on practical approaches to analysing volumetric image data, with examples drawn from quantitative imaging workflows used across research environments.

Participants will be introduced to current methods for preprocessing, segmentation, measurement, and interpretation of 3D imaging data. The sessions are designed to combine methodological context with hands-on discussion of real analysis challenges.
