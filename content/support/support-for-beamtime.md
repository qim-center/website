The Qim Center provides support to researchers planning and conducting beamtime experiments at synchrotron and laboratory X-ray. We can connect users with the relevant experts at imaging facilities.

## Pre-Beamtime Support

- **Experiment planning** — Advice on sample preparation, scan parameters, and data acquisition strategies.
- **Feasibility assessment** — Evaluation of whether your research question can be addressed using the available imaging techniques.

## Contact

To discuss beamtime support, contact us at [info@qim.dk](mailto:info@qim.dk).
