The Qim Center provides support to researchers planning and conducting beamtime experiments at synchrotron and laboratory X-ray sources. Our expertise spans experiment design, data acquisition, and post-experiment analysis.

## Pre-Beamtime Support

- **Experiment planning** — Advice on sample preparation, scan parameters, and data acquisition strategies.
- **Feasibility assessment** — Evaluation of whether your research question can be addressed with the available imaging techniques.
- **Proposal writing** — Assistance with preparing beamtime proposals and justifying your experimental approach.

## During Beamtime

- **On-site support** — We can provide remote or on-site assistance during your experiment.
- **Real-time feedback** — Quick assessment of acquired data to ensure quality and identify issues early.
- **Troubleshooting** — Help with acquisition parameters and unexpected challenges.

## Post-Beamtime

- **Data processing** — Reconstruction, preprocessing, and organization of acquired datasets.
- **Analysis** — Quantitative analysis of reconstructed volumes to extract results relevant to your research.
- **Long-term collaboration** — Ongoing support for data analysis and manuscript preparation.
