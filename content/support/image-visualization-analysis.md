  Volume Explorer
</a> which allows visualization of large OME-Zarr datasets directly in the web browser:

## Getting Started

If you have 3D imaging data that needs visualization or analysis, contact us at [info@qim.dk](mailto:info@qim.dk) to discuss your needs. 

We can provide access to our tools and platforms, or work directly with you on your dataset.
