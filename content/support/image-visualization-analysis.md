## Overview

Working with 3D imaging data requires powerful visualization and analysis tools. The QIM Center provides access to a suite of software for exploring volumetric datasets, identifying features of interest, and extracting quantitative measurements.

## What We Offer

- **Interactive 3D visualization** — Navigate through volumetric datasets, adjust contrast and opacity, and explore complex structures in three dimensions.
- **Quantitative analysis** — Measure volumes, surface areas, thickness distributions, and other morphological parameters from segmented data.
- **Image processing** — Apply filters, denoising, segmentation, and other preprocessing techniques to prepare data for downstream analysis.
- **Custom workflows** — We can help design and implement analysis pipelines tailored to your specific research questions.

## Getting Started

If you have 3D imaging data that needs visualization or analysis, contact us at [info@qim.dk](mailto:info@qim.dk) to discuss your needs. We can provide access to our tools and platforms, or work directly with you on your dataset.
